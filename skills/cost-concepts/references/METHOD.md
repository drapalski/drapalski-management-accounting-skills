# Cost Concepts & Behaviour: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Core formulas**

- Linear mixed cost: `Total cost = fixed cost + (variable cost per unit × activity)`.
- High–low variable rate: `(cost at high activity − cost at low activity) ÷ (high activity − low activity)`.
- High–low fixed component: `total cost − (variable rate × activity)`.

**Decision process**

1. Define the cost object and relevant time horizon.
2. Determine whether each cost changes under the proposed decision.
3. Identify capacity or range changes that could invalidate linearity.
4. Explain why accounting classifications can differ with the decision context.

**Worked synthetic example**

At 1,900 service transactions, monthly processing cost is €12,220. At 3,100 transactions, it is €16,660. The high–low variable rate is `€4,440 ÷ 1,200 = €3.70 per transaction`. Fixed cost is `€12,220 − (1,900 × €3.70) = €5,190`. Hence the estimated model is `€5,190 + €3.70 × activity`. Both observed points reconcile.

**Common traps**: fixed per period does not mean fixed at all volumes; sunk cost is not a future cash flow; direct is not synonymous with variable.
