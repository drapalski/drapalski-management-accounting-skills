# Cost Concepts & Behaviour: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — subscription support operations (synthetic)**

A service unit reports €9,960 of monthly cost at 1,600 requests and €13,440 at 2,400 requests.

1. Estimate the mixed-cost equation with high–low.
2. Forecast cost at 2,700 requests.
3. Explain one limitation of the forecast.

**Facilitator check**: variable rate `€3,480/800 = €4.35`; fixed component `€3,000`; forecast `€14,745`. The estimate assumes 2,700 requests remains within the relevant range.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
