# Activity-Based Costing: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — product complexity (synthetic)**

Two activities: setups cost €91,000 for 350 setups; inspection costs €64,800 for 1,800 inspections. Product A uses 110 setups and 670 inspections; Product B uses the remainder.

1. Compute both rates.
2. Allocate overhead to each product.
3. Reconcile the total.

**Facilitator check**: setup €260/setup, inspection €36/inspection. A `€28,600+€24,120=€52,720`; B `€62,400+€40,680=€103,080`; total €155,800.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
