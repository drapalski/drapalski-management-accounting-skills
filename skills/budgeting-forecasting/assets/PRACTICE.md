# Budgets, Forecasts & Scenarios: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — monthly operating plan (synthetic)**

Forecast sales are 6,200 units; opening finished units 470; targeted closing units 610. Each produced unit needs 2.5 kg of inputs. Raw materials open at 1,400 kg and should close at 1,750 kg. Input costs €3.20/kg.

1. Find the production budget.
2. Find materials purchases in kg and euros.
3. Explain one cash-flow factor not visible in these calculations.

**Facilitator check**: production `6,340 units`; materials needed `15,850 kg`; purchases `16,200 kg`; purchases value `€51,840` before tax, discounts and payment-timing adjustments.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
