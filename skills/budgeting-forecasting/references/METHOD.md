# Budgets, Forecasts & Scenarios: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Core formulas**

- `Required production = forecast sales + desired closing finished goods − opening finished goods`.
- `Required material purchases = production needs + desired closing raw materials − opening raw materials`.
- `Ending cash = beginning cash + cash receipts − cash payments`.

**Decision process**

1. Set unit conventions, period and inventory policy.
2. Build quantities before monetary values.
3. Respect settlement lags and payment timing.
4. Run base, downside and upside cases and verify roll-forward totals.

**Worked synthetic example**

Forecast sales: 4,700 units, opening stock: 380 units, desired closing stock: 520 units. Required production is `4,700 + 520 − 380 = 4,840 units`. Cash timing requires a separate receipts schedule rather than assuming all sales are collected immediately.

**Common traps**: mixing units and currency; reversing the inventory roll-forward; confusing accrual earnings with liquidity.
