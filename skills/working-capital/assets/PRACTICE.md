# Working Capital & Liquidity: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — distribution operation (synthetic)**

Average receivables €410,000; credit sales €3,280,000; average inventory €560,000; cost of sales €2,240,000; average payables €230,000. Assume 360 days.

1. Calculate DSO, DIO, DPO and the cash conversion cycle.
2. Suggest one measured operational improvement.

**Facilitator check**: DSO 45; DIO 90; DPO ≈ 36.96; CCC ≈ 98.04 days. Discuss seasonality and whether 360 or 365 days is the reporting convention.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
