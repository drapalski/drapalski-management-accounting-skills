# Working Capital & Liquidity: practical coaching prompts

All prompts use fictional companies and synthetic data.

1. **Explain and diagnose:** "Explain the method in plain English. Ask me a diagnostic question before solving an example."
2. **Find the assumption:** "Give me a scenario where a correct calculation leads to a poor decision. Identify which assumption fails."
3. **Model alternatives:** "Create two anonymized business choices, show the full numbers and let me choose a method before you reveal the comparison."
4. **Check my work:** "Review my reasoning and calculations line by line. Give hints first and then the answer if I request it."
5. **Executive summary:** "Summarize the recommendation, benefit, risk and next evidence needed in five sentences."
