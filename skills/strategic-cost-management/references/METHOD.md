# Strategic Cost & Value Decisions: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Framework**

1. State the objective: profit, cash, return, growth, resilience or service.
2. Map the value chain, strategic activities and cost drivers.
3. Identify relevant incremental economics and external constraints.
4. Compare at least two scenarios quantitatively, then assess execution risks.
5. Offer a recommendation contingent on explicit assumptions.

**Useful metrics**

- `Total contribution = units sold × (price − unit variable cost)`.
- `Simple incremental operating profit = incremental contribution − incremental avoidable fixed costs`.

**Worked synthetic example**

A product redesign saves €5.50/unit over 12,800 forecast units but costs €47,000 upfront for the decision horizon. Before other effects, incremental profit is `12,800 × €5.50 − €47,000 = €23,400`. Quality, demand and implementation effects are unquantified and should be modelled before approval.

**Common traps**: deciding on allocated average cost alone, ignoring lifecycle phases, omitting constraints and non-financial consequences.
