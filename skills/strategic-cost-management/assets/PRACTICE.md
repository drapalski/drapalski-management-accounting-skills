# Strategic Cost & Value Decisions: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — process modernization (synthetic)**

Option A lowers variable manufacturing cost by €4.80 for 18,500 forecast units but increases periodic fixed operating cost by €61,200.

1. Estimate incremental operating profit at forecast volume.
2. Calculate the volume at which savings cover the extra fixed cost.
3. List two factors that could reverse the recommendation.

**Facilitator check**: profit improvement `€88,800 − €61,200 = €27,600`; break-even `€61,200/€4.80 = 12,750 units`. Demand, quality, supplier risk, investment timing and cash constraints need assessment.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
