# Overhead Allocation & Full Costing: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Core formulas**

- `Predetermined overhead rate = budgeted overhead pool ÷ budgeted driver quantity`.
- `Applied overhead = rate × actual driver usage by cost object`.
- `Full manufacturing cost = direct material + direct labour + allocated manufacturing overhead` (subject to policy and scope).

**Decision process**

1. Define the cost object and eligible overhead pool.
2. Choose an allocation base connected to resource consumption.
3. Calculate a separate departmental rate when relevant.
4. Reconcile applied and actual overhead and explain limitations.

**Worked synthetic example**

A service department budgets €246,400 indirect cost and 15,400 processing hours. Its rate is €16 per hour. A job using 37 hours absorbs €592. The rate is not proof that €592 is avoidable cash cost.

**Common traps**: mixing actual costs with budgeted denominator in predetermined rates; using a rate without units; treating allocated cost as incremental.
