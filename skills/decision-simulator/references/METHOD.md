# Integrated Decision Simulator: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Integrated workflow**

1. Ask for business objective, alternatives, data table and constraints.
2. Separate facts from estimates; create an assumptions log.
3. Choose the minimum coherent model: contribution, allocation, cash timing and KPIs as needed.
4. Calculate one baseline, one downside and one upside case.
5. Reconcile units, currencies and periods.
6. End with a one-page recommendation: decision, economics, sensitivity, risks and next evidence to request.

**Worked synthetic example**

A service package yields contribution €27.50/unit, 3,600 units are expected and fixed operating costs are €72,000. Baseline operating profit is `€27.50×3,600−€72,000 = €27,000`. A 10% volume decline, holding all unit economics and fixed costs constant, reduces profit to `€17,100`. Recommend verifying whether any costs or prices change with volume before relying on the scenario.

**Common traps**: blending data from different periods; interpreting a plausible scenario as a forecast; hiding assumptions in text.
