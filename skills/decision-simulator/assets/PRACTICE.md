# Integrated Decision Simulator: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — combined management review (synthetic)**

A subscription service forecasts 5,600 subscriptions at €64 revenue and €37 variable service cost each; periodic fixed operating cost is €113,000. Receipts are 70% in-period and 30% next-period.

1. Compute forecast contribution and operating profit.
2. Compute cash receipts from current-period billings only (ignore prior period receipts).
3. Name two items needed for a complete cash forecast.

**Facilitator check**: contribution `5,600×€27=€151,200`; operating profit `€38,200`; current-period cash receipts `€250,880`. Prior receivables, payroll, capex, opening cash and supplier terms are missing.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
