# Performance, KPIs & Responsibility: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Useful calculations**

- `ROI = controllable operating profit ÷ operating assets` (define numerators and denominators explicitly).
- `Residual income = controllable operating profit − (capital charge rate × operating assets)`.
- `On-time delivery = deliveries on time ÷ total deliveries`.

**Decision process**

1. Write an objective and causal explanation before selecting KPIs.
2. Identify owner, data definition, measurement frequency and target.
3. Pair financial indicators with customer, process and capability indicators.
4. Audit controllability, gaming risk, timing lags and data integrity.

**Worked synthetic example**

A unit produces €184,000 operating profit on €1,150,000 operating assets. ROI is `16.0%`. At a 10.5% capital charge, residual income is `€184,000 − €120,750 = €63,250`. These two indicators answer related but different questions.

**Common traps**: averaging incompatible periods, ignoring capital employed, using activity metrics as substitutes for outcomes.
