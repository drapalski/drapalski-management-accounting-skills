# Performance, KPIs & Responsibility: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — service performance (synthetic)**

A team completed 2,360 tickets, of which 2,124 met the target deadline. It earned €146,000 operating profit on €920,000 operating assets; capital charge 11%.

1. Compute the on-time percentage and ROI.
2. Compute residual income.
3. Name one unintended incentive caused by rewarding only ticket volume.

**Facilitator check**: on-time = 90%; ROI ≈ 15.87%; residual income `€146,000−€101,200 = €44,800`.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
