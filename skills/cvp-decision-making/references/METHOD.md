# Contribution & CVP Decisions: method

All business illustrations and figures are synthetic.

**Core formulas**

- `Unit contribution = selling price − unit variable cost`.
- `Break-even quantity = fixed costs ÷ unit contribution`, provided unit contribution is positive.
- `Target-profit quantity = (fixed costs + target operating profit) ÷ unit contribution`.
- `Margin of safety = expected sales − break-even sales`; percentage is that difference divided by expected sales.

**Decision process**

1. Establish the time horizon, sales mix and relevant range.
2. Isolate incremental cash flows for the choice.
3. Add opportunity costs where capacity is constrained.
4. Report quantities rounded up to whole units when required.

**Worked synthetic example**

Price €73, unit variable cost €41, periodic fixed costs €51,200. Unit contribution is €32. Break-even is `51,200/32 = 1,600 units`. If expected sales are 2,050 units, margin of safety is 450 units or 21.95%.

**Common traps**: contribution is not gross profit; an allocated fixed cost is not automatically avoidable; a special order must consider displacement of normal demand.
