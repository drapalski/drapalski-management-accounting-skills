# Contribution & CVP Decisions: guided practice

All example organizations, scenarios and numeric figures are synthetic and anonymous.

**Scenario — digital services (synthetic)**

A business charges €92 per package, incurs €56 variable cost per package, and has €72,900 of fixed costs per period.

1. Compute break-even packages.
2. Compute packages needed for €18,000 operating profit.
3. Assess a proposed one-time order for 200 packages at €69 each when idle capacity exists and no extra fixed cost is incurred.

**Facilitator check**: unit contribution €36; break-even 2,025 packages; target profit 2,525 packages; incremental special-order profit `200 × (€69 − €56) = €2,600`, before any additional opportunity or fulfillment costs.

**Learning mode:** reveal facilitator checks only after the learner has attempted the question or asks for a complete answer.
