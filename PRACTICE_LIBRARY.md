# Practice lab — fictional scenarios

These short scenarios are newly authored and use **synthetic figures**. They do not describe any real organization or customer.

## Challenge A — prioritise capacity

Two products compete for 6,600 bottleneck minutes. Product A yields €21 contribution per unit and uses 3 minutes; Product B yields €29 contribution and uses 5 minutes. Maximum demand is A: 1,400 and B: 1,000 units. Assume divisibility only for ranking; final quantities must be whole and no set-up costs apply.

1. Rank by contribution per bottleneck minute.
2. Construct a feasible profit-maximising production plan under the specified constraints.
3. Explain what extra information would change the plan.

**Facilitator check:** A yields €7/minute and B yields €5.80/minute. Make 1,400 A (4,200 minutes) and 480 B (2,400 minutes); contribution €43,320. Consider other constraints and non-financial factors.

## Challenge B — working-capital sensitivity

Revenue is €4.62 million and average receivables €462,000. Use a 360-day reporting year and assume all revenue is credit sales. What would a 7-day reduction in DSO release, approximately, in cash?

**Facilitator check:** Daily sales = €12,833.33; release approximately €89,833.33, subject to no volume or credit-loss changes.

## Challenge C — multi-metric view

A unit generates €268,000 operating profit with €1.34 million of average operating assets. It has 84% customer retention versus an 88% target. Evaluate ROI and develop two balanced measures that prevent aggressive short-term cost cutting.

**Facilitator check:** ROI 20%. Measures may include retention, complaint resolution or service quality; targets and data quality need explicit definitions.
