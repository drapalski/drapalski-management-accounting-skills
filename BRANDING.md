# Brand system

**Publisher:** Drapalski Consulting  
**Product:** Management Accounting AI Skills  
**Positioning:** Practical, structured, AI-assisted financial decision learning.

## Visual identity

- **Palette:** black `#070709`, charcoal `#202026`, mid-gray `#6B6872`, hot pink `#F70786`, blush `#FFF3F8`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- **Logo:** use the supplied Drapalski wordmark unchanged, respecting its margins and proportions.
- **Style:** clean grid, ample white space, high-contrast headings, pink only as a meaningful focal point.
- **Typography:** Aptos, Inter or available system sans-serif; no fonts are packaged.
- **Voice:** precise, concise and decision-oriented; helpful without exaggerated claims.

## Editorial standards

All example organizations are anonymous and all case figures are **synthetic**. Use generic scenario labels rather than identifying external organizations or private customers. Keep context specific to the current product. Standard public-domain management accounting terminology is fine. Verify branding, image rights and professional claims before publication.
